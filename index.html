Hotel Bookings Overview in Portugal

Observations:

Que mostra aquest gràfic?

The plot shows how bookings have changed between a hotel in a city (City Hotel) and another one in a resort (Resort Hotel) from July 2015 to August 2017.

Y-axis shows the ammount of bookings.

X-axis shows the 4 countries with most bookings.

How can I navegate through the plot?

Use the slider under the plot to change of month and the bottons at the extremes to decrease or increase a year.

For each month there will be an observation made at the right panel.

Where does the data come from?

Data comes from the article: "Hotel booking demand datasets" written by Nuno Antonio, Ana de Almeida i Luis Nunes. Additionally, they dataset has been complemented with information from the following website 2010-2019: 40 important dates in Portugal which lists the major events in portugal between 2010 and 2019. Finally, the wikipedia page for the terrorist attack on france on November 2015 has been consulted.
